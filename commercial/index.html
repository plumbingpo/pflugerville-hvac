Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units have to replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Pflugerville HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service built around your hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and guests notice the drafts at the door and the heat right away. Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by written reports and service logs after each visit. That paperwork demonstrates a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and covers the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can create carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Every hour without climate control costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Pflugerville HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Pflugerville and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on rooftop and split systems. The priority is diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling fast. Once it's running, we put maintenance and monitoring in place so it doesn't happen again, and each response comes with full documentation for your records.
